💳 BTSBots On-Chain Instant Payment Integration Guide

Integrate zero-fee, instant crypto payments on BitShares blockchain.

💡 1. Payment Architecture & Workflow

Merchants generate order IDs with custom prefixes (e.g., exa_order_123). biz_bots listens for transfers, decrypting memos in real-time and posting signed notifications to pay_endpoint.

🔗 2. Standard Payment QR Scheme

btsbots://transfer?to=merchant_account&asset=CNY&amount=150&memo=exa_order_123
